    Hokkaido Flower Guide

    May Travel Face-Off: Mishima vs. Furano

    Planning a spring getaway? Here is exactly how Hokkaido's premier flower destinations look right now.

    Now In Season

    Kutchan, Hokkaido

    Mishima's Shibazakura Garden

    Peak Period: Late May - Early June
    Sweet Spot: May 22 – June 5

    This is the absolute best destination for a spring floral trip. Traveling right now provides a spectacular seasonal landscape contrast:

    • Stunning pink shibazakura (moss phlox) carpets
    • Fields of bright yellow canola flowers nearby
    • Clear views of snow-capped Mt. Yotei in the back

    Practical Tips:

    • Entry: Completely Free (Private property graciously open to the public).
    • Parking: No direct parking. Visitors utilize temporary off-site public lots.
    • Pro-Tip: Arrive between 7:00 AM – 9:00 AM for crystal-clear, cloudless mountain photos.
    Coming Soon

    Furano, Hokkaido

    Furano Lavender Gardens

    May Status: Too early for iconic lavender fields

    While you will encounter fresh green scenery and early spring flora right now, the postcard-perfect fields require waiting a bit longer:

    Late June - Early July

    Quieter crowds with early lavender blooms beginning to break alongside rows of poppies.

    Mid to Late July (Peak Bloom)

    The iconic window. Famous purple lavender carpets at Farm Tomita and rainbow lines are fully dynamic.

    Ideal Window for Your Travel Group:

    Target July 10 – 25 for the perfect combination of peak floral vibrancy and optimal photo weather.
